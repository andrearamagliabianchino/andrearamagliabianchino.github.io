## Selected projects

### The Interactive Mentor

*Universidad Politécnica de Madrid · HCI project, team of 4 · 2026*

A dual-screen assistant that guides older adults through mandatory digital public services. I proposed the concept and was the main contributor to the low- and high-fidelity prototypes. A moderated think-aloud test with 6 users aged 53–82 reached 100% task success and a SUS score of 78.75. The working MVP was built in React Native as a team; the design is entirely mine.

### PosiBuoy

*28Digital Summer School, ISEN Méditerranée · international team of 6 · 2026*

Eco-mooring buoys with environmental monitoring to protect Posidonia oceanica seagrass. I co-designed the booking app experience and co-developed the dashboard for marine park managers.

Live dashboard: <https://posi-buoy-guardian.vercel.app/>

### “Is it listening well?”

*Università di Trento · individual project · ongoing*

Real-time feedback on how well AI note-takers are capturing a meeting: distance, noise and overlapping speech. Problem defined from CHI and Interspeech research; next steps are critical-incident interviews and Wizard-of-Oz tests.
